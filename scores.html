How scoring works

  • Every status comes from a linked source. Nothing is estimated.
  • Met = 1 point. Partial = ½ point. Missing and Unknown = 0 points.
  • Unknown means no source was found. It does not mean the feature is missing.
  • The score is a verified minimum. Games with lots of unknowns may score higher once checked.
  • Coverage shows how much of the checklist has evidence. Under 25% is flagged.
  • Each game has one row per platform (PS4, PS5, PC, etc.). A source only counts for the platform it actually names; a source that doesn't specify counts for every version. This means one version can score much higher simply because it's the one that's been reviewed.
  • Sources were checked on 29 September 2026. Older sources are dated so you can judge them.

Game Accessibility Ratings (GAAR)

  • AAA (Exemplary) — Score 80+, Coverage 90%+. Gold-standard accessibility; industry benchmark. Examples: The Last of Us Part II (96/100, 100%).
  • AA (Highly Accessible) — Score 60–79, Coverage 70–89%. Strong accessibility across multiple dimensions; recommended tier. Example: God of War Ragnarök (72–74/100, 87–89%).
  • A (Accessible Basics) — Score 40–59, Coverage 40–69%. Foundation-level accessibility; playable with barriers. Most gaps are documented.
